How to Use GitMatch

1

Choose Your Tech Stack

Type keywords in the search bar (like FastAPI, React, or data cleaning) or click suggested tags, and pick your target programming languages.

2

Pick Your Experience Level

Beginner: Surfaces friendly starter tickets, documentation fixes, and unclaimed issues (0 comments).
Intermediate: Focuses on actual coding tasks (bug fixes, enhancements, refactors).
Advanced: Suppresses simple typo fixes and targets deep architecture, performance, and core tickets.

3

Interact & Save

Click Interested to bookmark an issue to your backlog and teach the recommender to surface more from that repository. Click Dismiss to remove it and immediately get a fresh replacement.

About GitMatch

GitMatch is an intelligent open-source issue recommender designed for computer science students and developers looking to make their first meaningful open-source contributions.

Instead of manually browsing through thousands of GitHub repositories, GitMatch builds a personalized user profile based on your tech stack, languages, and experience level, matching you to issues you can realistically solve.

Project Context: 3rd-Year Recommender Systems Course Mini-Project.
Stack: Python 3.13, FastAPI, Scikit-learn (Bigram TF-IDF), Cosine Similarity, GitHub REST API, Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS.
